I'm your Desk Buddy

I'm a little robot who lives on your desk. I blink, look around, get bored when you ignore me, nap, and wake up with brand new eyes. You don't need any buttons: just touch me, move me, or use my phone app.

Getting started

Let's be friends

I'm always on and run on my own battery. No power button: for dark and quiet, lay me on my face or switch me off in my app.

1

Find me a flat spot

I can feel which way is up, so a level desk keeps my eyes looking straight at you.

2

Charge me

Plug any USB-C phone charger into the socket on my back. I keep playing while I charge.

3

Get me online

The first time I start, I make my own Wi-Fi called Desk Buddy. Join it from your iPhone, a page opens, then pick your home Wi-Fi and type its password. I'll remember it.

4

Add my app

In Safari, open my page, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and type the code I show. That's where you set timers, reminders and alarms.

Without Wi-Fi I still blink, react and play. Wi-Fi gives me the right time, the weather, notes from Abijit and new tricks.

Touch me

Pokes, pets and shakes

My screen feels your finger, and a motion sensor inside feels every bump, lift and lean.

PokeA quick tap makes me squint. Five taps in 3 seconds and I'm grumpy for about 4 seconds, then I forgive you. Best friends get 8 taps.
PetKeep your finger on my screen for a second. I blush and hearts float up until you let go.
ShakeSpiral eyes and a wobbly mouth for 3 seconds. Whoa.
Pick upBig eyes for a second and a half. Picking me up also wakes me.

Leave me alone

After 30 minutes with nobody to play with I get bored. After an hour I nap and dim my screen. Touch me or pick me up to wake me.

Move me

Leaning, naps and the 8-ball

Lean me

Tip me left or right and my pupils roll toward the low side. Tip me back and I look down at you; tip me forward and I look up. Stand me level and I go back to looking around.

Quiet time

Lay me on my face and I fall asleep with my screen off. No reminders or alarms while I lie there. Stand me up and I wake with new eyes.

Upside down

Turn me over and I get grumpy and say "Flip me back!". I flip my face around so you can still read it.

Magic 8-ball

Think of a yes-or-no question. Tap my screen once, then shake me within 4 seconds. I get dizzy, then my answer appears.

Made for you

Notes, parties and growing up

Notes from Abijit Wi-Fi

Abijit can send you little notes from wherever he is. A heart pops up, then the note slides in for about 10 seconds. If I'm napping when it arrives, I keep it safe and show you when I wake up.

Birthday surprise

On your birthday, the first time I'm awake after 8 am, I put on a party hat and throw confetti. I celebrate my own birthday every year too: the day I became yours. song coming soon

We grow closer

Every pet and poke brings us closer, and pets count the most. I go from stranger to friend, close friend, best friend and soulmate, and I celebrate each time. The closer we are, the bigger my welcome when you come back, with more hearts. If you're away a whole day I miss you, and after a few days apart I fade a little, but never below halfway. My app shows how close we are.

Looking after me

Battery, care and new tricks

I'll tell you when I need charging. Plug a USB-C phone charger into my back; it's fine to leave me charging on the desk.

20% or lessA battery icon and sleepy eyes
5% or less"charge me", then I nap

Please be gentle with me

  • Keep me away from water and hot spots like a car dashboard or a sunny windowsill. My shell is PLA plastic, which softens in strong heat.
  • Wipe my screen with a soft, dry cloth, and tap it with a finger, not a pen or a nail.
  • Leave my back panel closed. My batteries and wires live in there.

New tricks over Wi-Fi

Abijit can teach me new tricks over Wi-Fi, so I never need opening. While I learn, a bar fills on my screen. Leave me standing until I restart by myself, usually within a minute.

Uh oh?

If something seems wrong

My screen is darkStand me up and tap my screen. Check I'm switched on in my app. Still dark? Charge me for a while.
My eyes are closed and my screen is dimI'm napping. Tap me or pick me up.
My eyes keep looking to one sideI'm leaning. Put me on a flat surface.
The time is wrongI set my clock over Wi-Fi. Check the Wi-Fi is on and give me a few minutes.
No weather when I wake upI need Wi-Fi. The weather can take up to 30 minutes to arrive.
I don't notice tapsPress with the pad of your finger. My screen feels pressure, not a light touch.
We moved somewhere with new Wi-FiAsk Abijit to reset my Wi-Fi, then do step 3 of Getting started again.

For anything else, ask Abijit.
